## About Page Outline (Rahmet Labs)

### Purpose
- Clarify that Rahmet Labs ships full-stack products (web, mobile, Web3) in addition to automation.
- Build trust via founder story, operating principles, proof, and social signals.
- Provide a clear CTA back into `/contact` and `/services`.

### Proposed Sections
1. **Hero / Mission**
   - Headline: “Rahmet Labs — automation-native product studio.”
   - Subhead: mention 50+ systems shipped, mix of web/mobile/web3/automation.
   - CTA buttons: `Configure a build` + `Talk to founders`.
2. **Who We Are**
   - Short bios for core team/founder, location, availability.
   - Photo collage or simple avatar grid (optional).
3. **Principles & Operating System**
   - 3–4 principles (e.g., “Telemetry-first”, “Design + Dev in one squad”, “Automation over headcount”).
   - Mention toolchain (Next.js, React Native, Solidity, n8n, LangChain, Supabase, etc.).
4. **Track Record / Metrics**
   - Reuse stats (50+ systems, 100% clients retained, 2× efficiency, 48h saved/week).
   - Add a timeline of major launches or logos (if cleared).
5. **Engagement Model**
   - Summarize Sprint / Launch / Partnership formats with links back to `/services`.
6. **Proof & Testimonials**
   - Pull quotes from portfolio entries or short anonymized victories.
7. **Contact / CTA Footer**
   - Language reminding visitors they can jump into the builder or DM directly.
