# DESIGN — Field Notebook

**THESIS.** The site is the engineer's field notebook, not a brochure: the same
document where chaos gets observed, diagrammed, and resolved into a system.
It refuses the dark-tech-void agency template and the cream-editorial cliché at
once — this is a *working document*, drawn, not decorated.

**OWN-WORLD.** Warm white paper, hairline rules, graph-paper grid where thinking
happens (hero, chart), a blue pen for the system being designed, a red pencil
for everything manual it replaces. Figure numbers (FIG. 01), margin annotations
in italic serif, stamps, and one hand-drawn thread that resolves from scribble
into a single clean line — that thread is the brand mark.

**STORY.** A founder lands and sees their day (scribble) become a drawing
(clean line) under their own scroll. They leaf through figures: what we build,
the manual-vs-system plot, shipped work as ledger entries, process as four
draft sheets. They close the notebook convinced and press one action: start.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** Graph-paper canvas, ink scribble resolving into a blue
engineered stroke behind a two-state headline ("drowning in manual work" →
"the systems that end it"), mono figure label, one blue pill action.

**FORM.** Light Persuade surface; scroll-scrubbed hero (PRODUCT.md hard
requirement), pinned chapters, horizontal process desk, ledger work index.

## Rules

- **Two inks only.** Blue = the system (CTAs, live, resolved line, active nav).
  Red = manual state, only ever opposite blue in a before/after. No third color.
- **Mono is measurement only.** JetBrains Mono for figure labels, data,
  timestamps, statuses — never as a "tech costume" on prose.
- **Paper texture comes from the subject:** graph grid, hairlines, margin
  rules. No generic gradient meshes, no glass decoration, no glow.
- **Cards are sheets:** 12px radius, 1px hairline, white on paper; recessed
  areas use the `well` tone. Depth = tone steps, shadows only as soft offset
  lifting, never colored halos.
- **Dark blue is one committed field** — the closing blueprint sheet (FinalCta)
  and only there at full-bleed scale.
- Full EN/RU/KZ parity; Geologica + Spectral + JetBrains Mono all carry
  Cyrillic and Cyrillic-extended glyph sets.
- Everything animates transform/opacity only; reduced-motion gets the full
  narrative as static figures.
