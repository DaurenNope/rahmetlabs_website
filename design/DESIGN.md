# DESIGN — The Crown (Kök)

**THESIS.** The site is a descent from day sky to night sky. Scattered ink
noise orders itself into the shanyrak — the sacred crown of the yurt, the
window that looks up at Tengri — and the crown then dissolves into what the
crown holds: a nomad family on the night pasture, herd around them,
21st-century tech (drone, solar panel, satellite) drawn as a constellation
map, the way nomads actually read the sky. Particles are native to night:
bone dots on ink are stars, never noise on a wall. Manual chaos is the noise;
the system is the crown; the point of the system is the flourishing home.

**OWN-WORLD.** Dusty daylit sky field `#3A5D72`, poster bone, warm
machine-black ink, and kyzyl red `#D93A1E` promoted to a single-moment
accent: tags, live dots, the tamga mark, and the closing field. Flat unmixed
color only; depth by overlap, never shading. The motif system comes from
ornament research, not stock vectors: the **shanyrak** crown with four
**tort kulak** horn spirals (the hero, as ~48k WebGL particles), **tumar**
amulet points on primary buttons, **shet-oyu** infinity borders at every
section seam, the **ilmek** square spiral as the tamga logo mark, and the
**Tamgaly** sun-head over the close. The four directions of the shanyrak
cross map to the four systems (N/E/S/W). Oswald monumental display (Russo
One failed Kazakh: no Қ Ң Ү Ө Һ Ә Ғ — Oswald carries full cyrillic-ext in
the same condensed monumental silhouette), Onest body, JetBrains Mono only
where real data lives (coordinates, languages, 4–12 weeks, ORDER %).

**STORY.** A founder watches a scattered day sky order itself into the crown
that holds the home; then day descends into night and the crown becomes the
home — family, herd, yurt, drone, solar panel, satellite — rendered as bold
filled silhouettes in the shyrdak felt-applique tradition, read as
constellations on the night field (particles excel at geometry and solid
form, never at figurative line art). The four directions of the crown become
the four systems; the mission log proves the team ships; the red field closes
with one amulet action.

**FIELD SYSTEM.** One field per chapter, never alternating: sky → dusk →
night (the myth, hero only) → bone (the explanation: thread + systems +
process + principles) → ink (the proof: work ledger + facts) → red (the
action: close). No green anywhere; ink is both the night and the proof
chapter — the same dark, two jobs.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** Full daylit sky field; monumental bone uppercase headline
left; a WebGL particle field right that assembles from ink noise into the
bone shanyrak, then morphs into the night-pasture constellation scene as the
field descends through dusk to night — all scrubbed, all viewports; real
telemetry at the edges; black tumar-point CTA; ORDER counter 0→100%.

**FORM.** Sky Persuade surface; pinned two-act scroll-scrubbed hero
(PRODUCT.md hard requirement, all viewports): act one chaos→crown with
headline flip at ORDER 100% while the camera pushes in, act two crown→night
pasture constellation scene while the camera pulls back to reveal the land.
Then one field per chapter: bone for the explanation (thread chapters,
systems plates, process timeline, principles grid), ink for the proof (work
ledger with real live captures, then the plain citable facts block for
AEO/GEO), red for the close (offer, tumar action, direct contact channels).
Signature moment: the full chaos→crown→home particle journey.

## Rules

- **Sky is the field, night is the pasture, red is the moment.** Sky carries
  act one; the field descends sky → dusk → night as the crown dissolves into
  the constellation scene; red appears only as tags, live dots, the tamga
  mark, and the full closing field. No yellow, ever; no green anywhere.
- **Flat unmixed color.** Depth by overlap (rotated plates, framed captures,
  ghost linework), never by shading, gradients, or glows.
- **Ornament is structure, not garnish.** Every motif carries meaning and a
  job: shanyrak = the system that holds; tort kulak = the four directions;
  tumar = protection (the ask); shet-oyu = continuity (seams); ilmek = the
  mark; sun-head = the close. No motif may appear without its job.
- **Mono is telemetry only.** JetBrains Mono for coordinates, directions,
  statuses, the ORDER counter — never as a tech costume on prose.
- **Particles are the material.** ~48k desktop / ~22k mobile, DPR capped at 2,
  render loop pauses off-screen and on hidden tabs. Chaos = ink noise on the
  sky; order = bone crown; a few hot white sparks at lock.
- **The crown never stops.** After lock it keeps a slow perpetual rotation
  (uRot), cursor repulsion stays live — the system is alive, not a poster.
- **Transitions travel, never lerp.** Particles move on curved swirl paths,
  the crown locks with a bright pulse wave, act two begins with a spin-up
  release, and the scene arrives as lift-and-rain; the field journeys
  sky → dusk → night in stages. The counter reads ORDER, then HOME.
- **The scene is silhouette, never line art.** The night-pasture tableau is
  sampled from solid filled forms (shyrdak applique language); the ambient
  steppe dust settles as the grass band; sky elements hover, the pasture
  ripples, everything shimmers like a constellation — on the night field
  that is literally what it is. Figurative outline illustration in particles
  is banned — it reads as children's drawing.
- **Real work only.** Ledger rows link to live URLs with real captured
  frames; no mockups, no invented metrics, no testimonials.
- Full EN/RU/KZ parity; Oswald + Onest + JetBrains Mono all carry
  Cyrillic and Cyrillic-extended glyph sets (verified against Kazakh).
- Everything animates transform/opacity only; reduced-motion gets the full
  narrative as static states (crown formed, both headline states stacked).

## Provenance

Direction rolled via concept-seed (orbital modernism), fused with the Kazakh
ornament research layer (tort kulak, tumar, shet-oyu, ilmek, Tamgaly) and
pinned by the user. Palette journey: kyzyl red field → night-sky Kök Tengri →
back to red → locked by the user at daylit Kök `#3A5D72` with red promoted to
accent, after living with each (earlier builds archived in
`references/scrap/`). Finish review passed with fixes applied: tort kulak
legibility at lock, mobile scroll-scrub, palette unification, the work→CTA
seam divider, mobile crown plane, real work captures. Reference mock:
`references/shanyrak.html`.
